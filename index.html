Privacy Policy

App: FileFlow: Image Converter
Effective Date: September 27, 2026
Last Updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FileFlow: Image Converter ("FileFlow", "we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the FileFlow Android application.

FileFlow is designed primarily for local file processing. The app provides image, PDF, OCR/scanner, and other document utilities without requiring a FileFlow account or uploading your personal files to a FileFlow-operated server.

Important: Your selected files are processed locally on your device where the applicable feature supports local processing. FileFlow does not operate a custom server for uploading or storing your personal files.

1. Information and Files You Provide

Depending on the features you use, FileFlow may access files that you intentionally select or create through the app, including:

  • Images such as JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP files.
  • PDF documents.
  • Text that you enter into the app.
  • Images captured using the document scanner.
  • Text extracted from images using OCR.
  • Generated files created by FileFlow during processing.

FileFlow accesses files only when you use a feature that requires them, such as selecting an image, opening a PDF, scanning a document, converting a file, or running OCR.

2. Local File Processing

FileFlow is designed to process supported files locally on your Android device. Features include image conversion, compression, resizing, cropping, PDF creation and processing, document scanning, OCR, file preview, and batch processing.

FileFlow does not intentionally upload your selected personal files to a FileFlow-operated cloud server for processing or storage.

3. Camera Permission

FileFlow may request camera permission when you use features such as Document Scanner or camera-based Image to Text.

Camera access is used to capture images or documents that you choose to process. Camera permission is not required for features that do not use the camera.

4. OCR and Text Recognition

FileFlow provides on-device OCR functionality for extracting text from supported images and scanned documents.

OCR processing is designed to occur locally on your device. Extracted text may be temporarily or persistently stored locally when you choose to save or keep an OCR result in FileFlow.

5. Generated Files and History

FileFlow may create processed files such as images, PDFs, and text files in the app's private storage.

FileFlow may also store local history information so that you can view and manage previously generated results. History information may include information such as the generated file name, file type, category, and local file reference.

Generated files and local history are stored on your device and are not intentionally uploaded to a FileFlow-operated server.

6. Offline Processing Limit

FileFlow may provide a limited number of processing operations when your device is offline. The current offline allowance is stored locally on your device and is reset according to the local calendar day.

This locally stored limit is not used to collect or transmit your personal files.

7. Advertising

FileFlow may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads services (AdMob).

When advertising services are enabled, Google Mobile Ads may collect and process certain information such as IP address, advertising or device identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for purposes such as advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. The exact information handled can depend on Google's current SDK configuration and applicable privacy choices.

Google provides information about data handled by its Mobile Ads SDK in its developer documentation: Google Mobile Ads data disclosure .

FileFlow uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to provide users with privacy and consent choices related to advertising.

If you purchase an active Remove Ads subscription, FileFlow disables the app's advertising display according to the subscription status provided by Google Play.

8. Remove Ads Subscription

FileFlow offers an optional monthly auto-renewing subscription called "Remove Ads".

Subscription purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. FileFlow does not receive or store your full payment card number, banking credentials, or other payment credentials.

Google Play manages the payment transaction, subscription renewal, cancellation, and related billing information according to Google's policies and terms.

9. Information Stored in App Settings

FileFlow may store certain settings locally on your device, including preferences such as theme settings, local processing allowance information, and other app configuration needed for the app to function.

These settings are stored locally and are not intentionally uploaded to a FileFlow-operated server.

10. File Sharing and Export

FileFlow allows you to save, share, copy, or export files and extracted text using Android's system functionality.

When you choose to share a file, the file is provided to the Android sharing mechanism and the application or service you select. The privacy practices of that third party then apply.

11. Third-Party Services

FileFlow may use third-party services and SDKs necessary to provide advertising, consent management, subscription billing, app functionality, or other platform services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Relevant services may include:

  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Play services
  • On-device machine-learning services used for supported OCR functionality

12. Data Security

FileFlow is designed to minimize unnecessary collection of user files and to process supported files locally whenever technically possible.

Files generated by FileFlow are stored in the app's private storage areas subject to Android's platform security controls. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

FileFlow does not maintain a FileFlow-operated cloud repository of your personal files.

Generated files and local history may remain on your device until you delete them, clear the relevant history, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or the app removes files according to its normal file-management behavior.

You can manage or delete generated files and history using the controls provided by FileFlow and Android.

14. Children's Privacy

FileFlow is a general-purpose file utility application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children for the purpose of maintaining a FileFlow account or profile.

15. Your Choices

Depending on the feature and your device settings, you can:

  • Choose which files FileFlow can access.
  • Grant or deny camera permission.
  • Delete generated files and local history.
  • Manage Android advertising and privacy settings.
  • Make available consent choices for applicable advertising services.
  • Purchase, cancel, or manage the Remove Ads subscription through Google Play.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when FileFlow's features, services, legal requirements, or data practices change.

When we make changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
